Ask Gemini for Safari

Chrome-style Ask Gemini overlay powered by your Gemini subscription — no API key required.

1

Enable Extension in iOS Settings

Open Settings → Apps → Safari → Extensions → Ask Gemini and turn on Allow Extension (and enable in Private Browsing if desired).

2

Allow Website Permissions

Under Ask Gemini's permissions, set both gemini.google.com and Other Websites to Allow so the mini button can read page context and talk to Gemini.

3

Sign In Once at gemini.google.com

Open gemini.google.com in Safari and sign into your Google / Gemini subscription. Then tap the mini ✨ button at the top-left of any website!

Pro tip: You can drag the mini top-left ✨ button up or down along the left edge on any website, or customize its size & transparency from Safari's Extensions menu.
